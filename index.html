Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information the STRYKE mobile game (Android and iOS) handles, why, and the choices you have. STRYKE is made by BAKIROV UMARALI ZOXIDJON-OGLI, YATT (Digital Group), Fergana region, Uzbekistan ("we", "us").

In short: the game works offline and keeps your progress on your device. We do not ask for your name, email or location, the game uses no ad network or tracking, and we collect no personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, unlocked weapons and rooms, records, missions, daily rewards, purchases), your settings and the player name you choose are saved only on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Ads for our own apps

The game shows ads only for other apps made by Digital Group: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus, and occasional full-screen ads between runs. These ads are part of the game itself. No ad network is used, no advertising ID is read, and nothing about you or your device is collected or sent when an ad is shown.

If you tap "Get it on Google Play", the app's Google Play page opens. The link tells Google Play that you came from STRYKE, so we can see how many installs our ads bring. What Google Play does is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Rewarded ads are always optional: they only show when you tap a reward button.
  • No Ads: buying "No Ads" in the shop removes the ads between runs. Rewarded ads stay optional.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation of what was bought, so the game can grant it and restore it.

5. Children

STRYKE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we keep no personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete; purchase records are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Contact us and we will help with any request.

7. Online features

Future versions may add cloud save, leaderboards, crash reports and analytics. We will update this policy before such features are released and say which services they use.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be shown in the game.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: info@digital-group.uz
